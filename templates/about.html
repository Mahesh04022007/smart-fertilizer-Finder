About Smart Fertilizer Finder

Smart Fertilizer Finder is an online fertilizer availability and purchasing system. It gives farmers one place to see what a fertilizer costs, whether it is in stock, and which nearby shop carries it.

Buying fertilizer often means asking several shops, guessing at the pack price, and travelling when a product is already finished. This website keeps the catalog, shop list, and orders in one database so the information stays consistent.

Find the product

Search urea, DAP, NPK, potash, organic fertilizers, super phosphate, micronutrients, and neem cake. Open a product for the description, price, usage notes, and stock.

Check a local shop

Shops are listed with the owner, village, address, phone number, and the fertilizers they keep. Search by village or town before you make the trip.

Purchase with an account

After registration, a farmer can add products to a cart, change quantities, and place an order. The order is saved and the stock count is reduced only if enough units are available.
